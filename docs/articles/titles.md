# Proposed Article Titles: High-Level Vision for the Autonomous Economy

Based on the feedback, these titles are geared toward Venture Capitalists, industry analysts, and enterprise leaders. They avoid technical implementations (no mention of Go, Django, NATS, or JetStream) and instead focus on massive paradigm shifts, market opportunities, economic incentives, and the strategic repositioning of the finance industry.

1. **The "AWS for Agents": Building the Foundational Infrastructure for the Autonomous Economy**
   *A thesis piece on why the next trillion-dollar company won't be an AI model, but the foundational protocol (AgentHub) that allows millions of AI agents to discover, negotiate, and transact securely with each other.*

2. **The Death of the Interface: Why API-First Agent Networks Will Swallow Traditional SaaS**
   *Argues that B2B SaaS interfaces are a legacy crutch for human operators, and the future belongs to headless, agentic protocols where software provisions itself.*

3. **From Data Clerks to Financial Architects: The Evolution of the CPA in the Agent Era**
   *A look at the human impact: as autonomous agents eliminate manual reconciliation, CPAs will ascend to higher-margin advisory roles, designing verification rules and monitoring real-time financial telemetry.*

4. **Algorithmic Trust: Replacing Corporate Credit with Cryptographic Execution Proofs**
   *Explores the economic implications of the Almanac: how trust in the A2A economy shifts from subjective brand loyalty and credit scores to deterministic, mathematical track records.*

5. **The Autonomous CFO: Unlocking Scalable Enterprise Advisory Through Real-Time Telemetry**
   *Focuses on Fignode's true value proposition: giving boutique firms the ability to charge enterprise retainers by autonomously monitoring client cash flows, AR/AP, and runway 24/7.*

6. **The End of the Month-End Close: Designing Continuous Accounting for Real-Time Markets**
   *Discusses the shift from delayed, batch-processed financial reporting to continuous, real-time ledgers maintained by autonomous agents reaching instant consensus.*

7. **Pricing Certainty: How AI Agents Will Trade Verification in a Dynamic Spot Market**
   *Expands on the concept of "Reconciliation Markets," exploring how agents will dynamically negotiate the cost and depth of proof required for high-stakes vs. low-stakes transactions.*

8. **Zero-Friction Procurement: When Supply Chains Negotiate Themselves in Milliseconds**
   *A visionary piece on the future of B2B commerce, where corporate purchasing agents scan the network for the best vendors, negotiate terms, and execute contracts without a single human meeting.*

9. **The Executable Ledger: Moving from Static Records to Live Agent Consensus**
   *Contrasts traditional, dead databases with the Toro Protocol's active ledger, where financial truth emerges from the ongoing, cryptographically secure interactions of fiduciary agents.*

10. **Native Agentic Infrastructure vs. Bolted-On AI: Finance's True Pivot Point**
    *A strategic breakdown of why existing ERP systems adding "AI chatbots" will lose to natively designed agentic networks that reconstruct double-entry bookkeeping from first principles.*
