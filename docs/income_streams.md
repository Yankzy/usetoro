# 1. Cash Flow Acceleration (you get paid for speed)

Businesses hate waiting to get paid.

### What you offer

* “Get paid instantly instead of 30 days”

### How it works

* Invoice = €10,000
* You advance €9,700 immediately
* You collect full €10,000 later

### Your profit

* €300 (3%)

You partner with a lender (don’t use your own capital).

This is exactly what Square does with merchant cash advances.

---

# 2. Late Payment Recovery (you get paid for chasing money)

Most SMEs are terrible at collecting payments.

### What you build

* Automated reminders
* AI follow-ups
* Escalation workflows

### Pricing

* % of recovered money (5%–15%)
  or
* Fixed fee per recovered invoice

You’re turning:

> “unpaid invoices” into a revenue stream

### What you offer

### Monetization

* Card interchange fees (tiny but scalable)

# 4. Tax Optimization Layer

This one is subtle but very valuable.

### What you build

* AI that suggests:

  * deductible expenses
  * tax-saving strategies
  * alerts before filing

### Monetization

* Premium feature (€50–€200/month)
* Or % of tax saved (more aggressive model)

Businesses will pay for:

> “You saved me €10K in taxes”

# 5. Compliance-as-a-Service

Accountants spend time on boring compliance.

* Incoming money

# The real pattern (this is what matters)

All of these come from 4 core surfaces:

1. **Money moving** → take a %
2. **Work being automated** → charge subscription
3. **Risk being reduced** → charge premium
4. **Decisions being improved** → sell insights

---

# Brutal prioritization (don’t get distracted)

If you try to build 12 things, you’ll build nothing.

Start with:

1. Payments (invoice + supplier)
2. Subscription (core product)
3. Compliance (sticky layer)

Then expand.
