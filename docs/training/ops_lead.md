**DATE:** April 8, 2026

**SUBJECT:** 90-Day Onboarding Syllabus: QA & AI Data Operations Lead

**SITUATION:**
As we scale the Toro OS LedgerLock engine and prepare for our Phase 2 developer cohorts, our primary bottleneck will shift from *writing* code to *testing* code and *training* our AI classification models. We are internally training a dedicated QA Sniper and AI Operator. This role prioritizes raw intelligence, pattern recognition, and UX stress-testing over traditional engineering credentials. 

**LANGUAGE PROTOCOL:** The trainee is native French-speaking. All Month 1 conceptual training will be conducted via French resources. Starting Month 2, LLMs (Claude/ChatGPT) will be utilized as the operational bridge to translate her French logic into English engineering tickets.

---

#### **THE 90-DAY "QA SNIPER" SYLLABUS**

**MONTH 1: DOMAIN PHYSICS (ACCOUNTING & LEDGER LOGIC)**
*Goal: Understand the rules of the game we are playing.*
* **Core Concepts (via French YouTube/Coursera):** Double-entry bookkeeping, the relationship between a General Ledger, Balance Sheet, and P&L. 
* **The Toro OS Workflow:** How a Stripe payout physically moves from a bank feed into a categorized ledger entry. 
* **Success Metric:** Trainee can successfully look at a raw bank statement and explain (in French) how three different transactions should be treated by an accountant.

**MONTH 2: AI PROMPTING & THE TRANSLATION BRIDGE**
*Goal: Weaponize LLMs to bypass the English language barrier.*
* **AI as an Exoskeleton:** Training her to write detailed, complex observations in French, and prompting Claude/ChatGPT to translate them into perfectly formatted, professional English.
* **Issue Tracking (Linear/Jira):** How to read a Kanban board. How to take her AI-translated English bug reports and file them directly into the engineering queue. 
* **Success Metric:** Trainee can find a simulated visual bug in the app, write the report in French, use AI to translate it, and assign a perfectly written English ticket to a developer.

**MONTH 3: THE SANDBOX (DESTRUCTION & ANNOTATION)**
*Goal: Hands-on stress-testing and data model training.*
* **Chaos Testing:** Trainee is given access to the Toro OS Staging Environment. Her only job is to try and break it. Upload wrong files, click "submit" 50 times in a row, swipe the wrong way. 
* **Data Annotation:** When the Toro OS AI fails to categorize a weird transaction (e.g., "GCP*CLOUD 8839"), she manually reviews the data, researches it, and assigns the correct category to train the model.
* **Success Metric:** Trainee files her first 20 valid bug reports and successfully annotates 500 edge-case transactions.

#### **POST-GRADUATION RESPONSIBILITIES (MONTH 4 & BEYOND)**
Upon successful completion of the 90-day syllabus, she will officially assume the role of **QA & AI Data Operations Lead**. Her daily responsibilities will include:

1.  **The "Brenda" Reality Check:** Before any new feature built by the Accelerator Cohorts is pushed to Robert's U.S. CPA clients, she must test the UI. If it is not intuitive to her, the UI is rejected and sent back to the developers. 
2.  **Human-in-the-Loop Labeling:** Managing the master dashboard of unclassified ledger transactions. She is the final human authority that corrects the AI's categorization mistakes, continuously making the LedgerLock engine smarter.
3.  **Release Gatekeeper:** No code goes to production without her spending 4 hours trying to physically break it in the staging environment.
