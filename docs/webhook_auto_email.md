## What We Do

We sit **between third-party event sources and your systems**.

Instead of integrating directly with every provider, you send events through our ingestion layer. We handle reliability, normalization, delivery, and replay, then forward clean, deterministic data to wherever you want it to go.

Destinations can be:

* Your production API
* A local development server
* A database you control
* Multiple endpoints at once
