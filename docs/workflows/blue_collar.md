To find the perfect industries for this model, you need to look for businesses that suffer from **high-volume communications** or **repetitive data entry**, and are run by **non-technical owners** who have cash but no time. 

Here are four incredibly lucrative vertical "Businesses in a Box" you can build on Toro OS and hand off to a new operator tomorrow:

---

### 1. Property Management & Real Estate (The "Midnight Maintenance" Engine)
Property managers are drowning in texts, emails, and phone calls from angry tenants, usually after hours.
* **The Workflow:** You build an AI Voice and SMS agent that handles maintenance requests. If a tenant texts, "My toilet is leaking," the AI Intent Agent routes it to the "Emergency Plumber Dispatch" workflow. If they ask, "When is rent due?", the AI checks the database and replies instantly.
* **The Operator Pitch:** You find a friend in real estate. You tell them: *"I built an AI Property Manager. It fields 100% of tenant maintenance calls and qualifies real estate leads. You sell it to property management firms for $399/month. You keep the profit; I charge you my $0.10 wholesale toll on the texts."*

### 2. Med Spas & Dental Clinics (The "Missed Call" Engine)
In high-ticket local healthcare (Med Spas, Dentists, Chiropractors), a missed phone call is a lost $1,000 appointment. Receptionists cannot answer the phone when they are checking out patients.
* **The Workflow:** You deploy the AI Receptionist. It answers overflow calls, answers FAQ about pricing (e.g., "How much is Botox?"), and sends an SMS link to the clinic's calendar to book the appointment.
* **The Operator Pitch:** You find an operator who is good at sales (or someone who already does marketing for Med Spas). They sell this as an "AI Front Desk" for $499/month. Clinics will happily pay this because saving just *one* missed patient a month covers the cost.

### 3. Freight Dispatch & Logistics Brokers (The "Where is my Truck?" Engine)
Logistics is an industry built entirely on messy data and endless phone calls. Brokers spend 80% of their day calling truck drivers asking, "Where are you?" and manually typing Bill of Lading data into spreadsheets.
* **The Workflow:** * *Data Ingestion:* Brokers drag and drop messy CSVs of shipping manifests into Toro OS. Your CSV primitive cleans and maps them instantly.
    * *Outbound SMS:* An automated workflow triggers an SMS to drivers at 8:00 AM asking for their location. The AI parses the driver's messy text response and updates the broker's database.
* **The Operator Pitch:** You hand this to someone who understands logistics. They charge trucking fleets $500/month for an "Automated Dispatch Assistant." 

### 4. Immigration & Personal Injury Law (The "Client Onboarding" Engine)
Lawyers charge hundreds of dollars an hour, but their paralegals waste weeks chasing clients to fill out intake forms, collect documents, and answer basic status questions.
* **The Workflow:** You build an AI WhatsApp/SMS agent that walks new legal clients through their onboarding checklist. *"Hi Maria, we still need a copy of your passport. Please reply to this message with a photo."* * **The Operator Pitch:** You find an operator connected to the legal space. They sell this to small law firms as a "Virtual Paralegal" for $500 to $1,000/month (lawyers are highly price-insensitive if it saves them billable hours).

### The Architecture of a New Vertical

Because you have built Toro OS with strict primitives (Switches, Circuit Breakers, CSV normalizers), launching one of these is just a matter of swapping out the context. 

1. **Change the Prompt:** Instead of *"You are a helpful plumbing assistant,"* you change it to *"You are a dental scheduling assistant."*
2. **Change the Target Data:** Instead of mapping QuickBooks Chart of Accounts, you map a shipping manifest CSV.
3. **Change the Operator:** Give the keys to a new LLC, set up their Master Tenant wallet, and collect your tolls.
