Here are three highly lucrative white-collar "Businesses in a Box" you can hand to an operator today:

---

### 1. The Recruiting Agency "Pipeline" Engine
Recruiters get 500 applicants for a single job. They spend hours staring at PDFs and spreadsheets to find the 5 people actually worth interviewing. 
* **The Workflow:** * **Node 0:** The recruiter exports a messy CSV of 500 applicants from LinkedIn or Indeed and drops it into Toro OS.
    * **The AI Filter:** Your Intent Agent scans the data for specific keywords (e.g., "5+ years Go experience").
    * **The Switch:** If `score > 80`, route to the "Interview" lane. If `score < 80`, route to "Rejection."
    * **The Action:** The system automatically sends a polite WhatsApp/SMS to the top 5 candidates with a Calendly link, and sends a polite automated rejection email to the other 495.
* **The Operator Pitch:** An operator sells this to boutique recruiting firms for $1,000/month. The pitch: *"I will automate your entire top-of-funnel screening so your recruiters only talk to pre-qualified candidates."*



### 2. The B2B Sales / RevOps "CRM Hygiene" Engine
Sales Account Executives (AEs) are notorious for refusing to update Salesforce or HubSpot. They leave deals in the wrong stage, which destroys the company's revenue forecasting. 
* **The Workflow:**
    * **The Ingestion:** An AE drops their raw post-call meeting notes (or a transcript) into a Slack channel connected to Toro OS.
    * **The AI Router:** The AI extracts the budget, the decision-maker, and the next steps. 
    * **The Circuit Breaker:** If the AE forgot to ask about budget, the Circuit Breaker hits a hard stop and pings the AE back in Slack: *"Missing Budget. Please reply with the client's budget before I update Salesforce."*
    * **The Action:** Once complete, it pushes perfectly formatted data into the CRM via API.
* **The Operator Pitch:** An operator sells this to VP of Sales at mid-market tech companies for $1,500/month. The pitch: *"I will give you perfect CRM data and accurate forecasts without forcing your reps to do data entry."*

### 3. The Wealth Management "Client Onboarding" Engine
Financial Advisors require massive amounts of documentation to legally onboard a new client (KYC - Know Your Customer). Clients drag their feet, and the onboarding takes weeks.
* **The Workflow:**
    * **The Ingestion:** The advisor triggers the workflow with the client's name and phone number.
    * **The Action:** The AI automatically texts/emails the client requesting their ID, W-2s, and bank statements.
    * **The Switch:** As the client replies with PDFs, Toro OS scans them. If a document is blurry or missing, it routes to a polite follow-up text: *"Hi John, that photo of your ID was a bit blurry, could you re-send it?"*
    * **The Circuit Breaker:** Once all 5 required documents are collected and verified, the workflow pauses and alerts the Financial Advisor to do the final human review. 
* **The Operator Pitch:** An operator sells this to independent financial advisory firms. Advisors will easily pay $800/month because faster onboarding means they start collecting management fees on the client's money weeks earlier.
